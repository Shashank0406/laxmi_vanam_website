Before check-in · Bhuvanagiri, Telangana

Guest rules & agreement

Please read all the rules carefully before check-in. By signing below, you acknowledge and agree to follow them during your stay at Laxmi Vanam.

Please note: No smoking indoors, no indoor cooking except tea and baby food, and no undeclared occupants. Serious violations may result in the security deposit being withheld or the booking being cancelled.

Important rules

01

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the farmhouse and all indoor areas. If any guest is found smoking indoors, the security deposit will not be refunded.

02

Cooking is permitted only in the designated outdoor kitchen. The only indoor exceptions are making tea and preparing food for babies or infants.

03

Only the number of guests declared at booking may stay. Any additional or undeclared guests must be informed to and approved by management before entering the property.

04

Guest ID proof

For check-in and security, please provide 3–4 valid government-issued ID proofs from the occupants staying at the property.

Damages, deposit & conduct

₹

Damages & security deposit

Damage to furniture, fixtures, appliances, the pool, property, or other amenities may result in a deduction from the security deposit. The deposit may be fully withheld in case of serious damage or violation of property rules.

!

Drugs and illegal substances, gambling, and verbal or physical fights are strictly prohibited. If an altercation occurs, management reserves the right to cancel the booking immediately without refund. Guests must obey all applicable local laws.

Pool safety

05

Glass bottles, glasses, and all other glass items are strictly prohibited in the pool area.

06

Diving into the pool is strictly prohibited for safety reasons.

07

Rain dance

The rain dance facility is available subject to water availability.

Property & nature

08

Dispose of waste in the designated garbage bins. Please do not litter around the trees or natural areas of the property.

09

The property is surrounded by natural greenery. Exercise caution, as snakes, insects, and other wildlife may occasionally be present.

10

Keep doors & windows closed

Keep doors and windows closed whenever possible to help prevent mosquitoes and insects from entering the farmhouse.

Noise & surroundings

11

Loud music is not permitted because the property is near temple premises. Please maintain a peaceful environment and respect the surrounding area.

Payment & check-in

12

The complete booking amount must be paid before check-in.

13

Guests are requested to provide 3–4 valid government-issued ID proofs for the occupants staying at the property.

14

Declared occupancy

Only the declared number of occupants are permitted. Any additional or undeclared occupants must be informed to management and approved before their arrival.

Security

15

Night-time gate closure

For security, please communicate with the caretaker regarding closing the main gate at night.

16

Lost belongings

Management is not responsible for lost, misplaced, or unattended personal belongings.

Cleaning & additional charges

17

A ₹1,000 cleaning charge will apply in case of vomiting inside the property or any situation requiring additional cleaning.

18

Up to 20 units: ₹500 minimum charge. More than 20 units: ₹1,000 charge.

Decorations & external services

19

Do not stick balloons, tape, hooks, adhesives, or other decorative materials directly onto walls or painted surfaces.

20

Third-party decorations & services

Inform management and obtain approval in advance for third-party decorations, catering, photography, events, or other external services.

Water, kitchen & power

21

Water availability

Water supply is currently not provided at the farmhouse. Please arrange drinking water and other water requirements before arrival.

22

Utensils & cutlery

Only large cooking utensils are provided. Cutlery such as spoons, forks, and knives is not provided.

23

Power backup

An inverter provides backup power during an outage. Backup capacity may be limited depending on usage.

Guests are expected to maintain the cleanliness, safety, and peaceful environment of Laxmi Vanam and ensure all members of their group follow these rules.

Management reserves the right to take appropriate action, including withholding the security deposit or cancelling the booking, in case of serious violations.
