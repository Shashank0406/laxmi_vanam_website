The stay

A farmhouse for gatherings, not a hotel corridor.

Guests come here for the air, the water, and the quiet. Days typically look like pool time, a walk through the greenery, and an evening sit-out under the shed while the fields stay green around you.

Swarnagiri Temple is close enough that a morning visit is part of many weekend plans — several guests mention the shikhara darshan from the property area.
